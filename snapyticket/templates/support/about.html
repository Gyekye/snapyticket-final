About Evesbay


Our Story

Welcome to Evesbay Inc, your number one source for all things ticket. We're dedicated to providing you the very best of event tickets, with an emphasis on stress-relief, simplicity and customer satisfaction. Founded in 2020, Evesbay Inc has come a long way from its beginnings in Accra, Ghana. When Evesbay first started out, the passion for web development drove the founders to start their own business. We hope you enjoy our services as much as we enjoy offering them to you. If you have any questions or comments, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Aims and Ambition

Ever thought that your tickets can get missing after purchase? Evesbay aims at eliminating hard copy tickets to events in the entertainment industry. The idea of using QR-based tickets makes it very simple to use and the risk of loosing the ticket is very low since it is QR. Individuals go through a lot of stress just to purchase a ticket and Evesbay aims to make purchasing tickets very simple from your own comfort zone.

Goals and Targets

Hardwork, determination and persistence leads to success. Evesbay targets loyal customers and event organizers to help them expand their reach to audience and attendees at their events. With the QR-based tickets initiative, purchasing tickets will be stress-free and simple. A user-friendly UI experience in addition helps users to use Evesbay very easily.
