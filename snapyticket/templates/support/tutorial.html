Evesbay Tutorial



Steps

1. Create an account on evesbay and register as either a buyer or an event organizer.

2. Browse the various events on the pages or search the event of your choice.

3. Add the selected event to your ticket bag and proceed to checkout.

4. Proceed to payment and add your payment details to purchase the tickets.

5. View your purchase tickets at your orders page and attend your favourite events.
